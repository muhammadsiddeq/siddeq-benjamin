About Me

Benjamin Hardman is an Australian-born photographer and natural history cinematographer currently based in Reykjavik, Iceland.

His work focuses on documenting the raw beauty, extreme weather, and wildlife of the Arctic and Polar Regions through atmospheric visual storytelling.

Over the years, he has collaborated on international natural history documentaries, conservation expeditions, and commercial film productions in sub-zero environments.
